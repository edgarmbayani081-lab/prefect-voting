How to vote

  1. Open Student Login and enter your Voter Code and password from your slip.
  2. Read each candidate’s profile and manifesto.
  3. For each of the fifteen positions, press Vote on your choice (or choose Abstain). For Dining Prefects you may choose up to the number shown.
  4. Press Review & Submit, check your choices, confirm, and submit.
  5. You will see a confirmation. Votes cannot be changed after submission.

Voting rules

  1. One voter account per student. Do not share your password.
  2. You may vote only once for each position.
  3. Your choices are stored separately from your name — administrators see turnout, never who you voted for.
  4. Results are not shown to students while voting is open.
  5. Lost your slip? Ask the election officer for a replacement.
